Slap Ya mama chicken marinade

Chicken marinade

Prep
15 min
Total
15 min
Serves
8

Ingredients

  • 2 cups roasted peppers (skin peeled)
  • 1 large onion, roughly chopped
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • ¼ cup white wine vinegar
  • 2 tbsp rib rack chicken seasoning
  • 2 tsp chica licka bam bam seasoning
  • 1 cup olive oil
  • ½ cup Greek yogurt (two generous dollops)
  • 2 squirts of ketchup

Method

  1. Blend everything together in a blender or food processor, except the Greek Yogurt
  2. Once blended add the greek yogurt and mix
