Tandoori Butter Chicken

Tandoori butter chicken, smooth, creamy, and way less clove-forward. Made with thighs and Greek yogurt.

Prep
15 min
Cook
45 min
Total
1 hr
Serves
8

Ingredients

  • 3 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 6 tablespoons butter
  • 2 medium onions, diced
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tablespoons fresh ginger, minced
  • 3 cans tomato sauce (15 oz each)
  • 3 teaspoons tandoori masala
  • 2 teaspoons paprika
  • 1 teaspoons cumin
  • 1 teaspoons chili powder
  • 1.8 cups plain Greek yogurt (full fat)
  • 1 pinch salt and black pepper
  • 3 tablespoons oil for cooking

Method

  1. Sear the thighs: Season 3 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces generously with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat 3 tablespoons oil for cooking in a large pan over medium-high heat. Working in batches, sear the chicken 10 minutes per side until golden. Set aside.
  3. Build the base: In the same pan, melt 6 tablespoons butter. Add 2 medium onions, diced and cook 6 minutes until soft and caramelizing. Stir in 6 garlic cloves, minced and 2 tablespoons fresh ginger, minced, cooking 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
  4. Bloom the spices: Add 3 teaspoons tandoori masala, 2 teaspoons paprika, 1 teaspoons cumin, and 1 teaspoons chili powder to the pan. Stir constantly for 45 seconds to bloom the spices without burning.
  5. Simmer the sauce: Pour in 3 cans tomato sauce (15 oz each). Stir well and bring to a simmer. Let it bubble gently for about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally to let flavors develop.
  6. Cook the chicken: Return all 3 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces to the pan. Simmer gently for 15 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and tender.
  7. Finish with yogurt: Remove from heat. Stir in 1.8 cups plain Greek yogurt (full fat) gently until combined, don't boil or it'll curdle. Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve over rice or with naan.
