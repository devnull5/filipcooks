Speca me mazë

Roasted peppers simmered in a creamy goat feta sauce seasoned with Vegeta.

Prep
5 min
Cook
14 min
Total
19 min
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cups sour cream
  • 1 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cups goat feta cheese, crumbled
  • 2 teaspoons Vegeta seasoning
  • 9 roasted peppers, skins removed
  • 0.3 cups pepper water (liquid from the roasted peppers)
  • 1 teaspoons olive oil

Method

  1. Make the cream base: In a bowl, combine 0.5 cups sour cream sour cream, 1 cups heavy cream heavy cream, and 1 cups goat feta cheese, crumbled goat feta cheese. Mix until well combined, then season with 2 teaspoons Vegeta seasoning.
  2. Prep the peppers: Take 9 roasted peppers, skins removed roasted peppers from the fridge and peel off the skins.
  3. Cook the peppers: Place the peppers in a deep pan. Pour in 0.3 cups pepper water (liquid from the roasted peppers) pepper water and 1 teaspoons olive oil olive oil. Cook over medium-high heat for 5 minutes
  4. Add the cream: Pour the cream base into the pan with the peppers. Cook for 8 minutes, stirring as needed, until the sauce is thickened and it's done.
