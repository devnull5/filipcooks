Cucumber onion tomato salad

Simple cucumber, tomato, onion salad

Prep
15 min
Total
15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 large cucumber
  • 2 medium tomatoes
  • 1 sweet yellow onion
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 bottle cap of vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon of Vegeta

Method

  1. Dice tomatoes
  2. Slice cucumber into thin slices
  3. Dice the onion
  4. Combine the vegetables together and mix
  5. Add a drizzle of olive oil and mix then add some more
  6. Sprinkle a small bit of white wine vinegar and mix it between drizzles
  7. Sprinkle a generous amount of Vegeta and mix between sprinkles
