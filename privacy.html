Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 September 2026

Filip Cooks is a personal recipe site. You can read every recipe without signing in or giving up anything. If you want to leave a review, you sign in with Google, and that means some data gets stored. This page says exactly what, where, and how to remove it.

What gets collected

If you just read recipes

Nothing. No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts beyond the two the site needs to function (a JavaScript library and a web font, listed below). No cookies are set.

If you sign in to review

DataWhere it comes fromWhy
Your nameYour Google accountShown next to your review
Your profile pictureYour Google accountShown next to your review
Your email addressYour Google accountIdentifies your account so you can edit your own review
Your rating and review textYou, when you write itIt's the point of the site

Your name, picture, rating and review text are public — visible to anyone who visits the recipe you reviewed. Please don't write anything there you wouldn't want publicly attached to your name.

Your email address is never shown publicly. It's stored so the site can tell which review is yours.

No passwords are ever collected. Sign-in happens entirely on Google's side; this site only receives the details in the table above.

Where it's stored

  • Supabase hosts the database holding recipes, reviews and profiles, and handles sign-in. Their privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy
  • GitHub Pages serves the pages themselves. GitHub receives your IP address as part of any ordinary web request, as every web host does. Their statement: GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Google handles authentication when you choose to sign in: policies.google.com/privacy
  • jsDelivr and Google Fonts serve a JavaScript library and a font file. These are ordinary asset requests.

What's kept in your browser

When you sign in, your login session is stored in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in between visits. It's readable only by this site, it never leaves your device except to authenticate you with Supabase, and signing out removes it. Clearing your browser data also removes it.

What is never done

  • Your data is never sold.
  • Your data is never shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • You'll never be emailed marketing. There is no mailing list.
  • Your behaviour across other websites is not tracked.

Deleting your data

  • A review: sign in, open the recipe, and use Delete my review. It's gone immediately.
  • Everything: email the address below and ask. Your profile and all your reviews will be deleted. No hoops.

Reviews may also be removed by the site owner if they're abusive, spam, or off-topic.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and accounts aren't knowingly created for them. If you believe a child has signed in, email the address below and the account will be removed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes to how review data is handled will be noted on the home page.
